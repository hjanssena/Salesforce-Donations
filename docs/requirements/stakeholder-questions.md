# Stakeholder Questions

Questions about how the program raises and handles donations for Mexico Dreamin today. The open questions come first, followed by the answers received so far from the stakeholder.

---

## Open Questions

### Donors and giving

1. Besides an email address, what else do you want to know about each donor, for example whether they are an alum, a family member or a company?
2. If someone wants to stay anonymous, should your own team still be able to see who they are?
3. If the same person gives twice by accident, what would you want to happen?
4. Do donations arrive outside the website?

### Public thanks

1. Can donors choose the name shown in public, such as a company or a family name?
2. Who decides whether a name is appropriate to display, and how many names should be shown at once?

### Tax invoices

1. When a donor asks for an invoice, who would deal with the request, and how does the donor receive it?

### Proof of the donation

1. Is the ticket the payment gateway emails enough as proof, or do you want one from the program as well? In which language should it be written?

### Campaigns

1. When does a campaign start, and who decides when a campaign is active, inactive or completed?
2. Besides its name, goal and amount raised, what should visitors see about each campaign, such as a description, images, a deadline or what the money is for?
3. When a campaign reaches its goal, who decides whether it keeps receiving donations, and based on what?
4. Should completed campaigns stay visible to visitors as a record of past fundraising?
5. Could your team change a campaign's goal after donations have come in, whether raising it or lowering it below the amount already raised?

### Donations that do not go through

1. How should we handle refunds or mistaken payments? Who approves them, and should a confirmed donation ever be undone?
2. Today, how does your team check that a donation was actually paid, and what would you do if the payment processor showed one thing and your records another?

### Your team

1. Who should be able to see donors' email addresses, and who may change a donation after it is recorded?

### General

1. In which language should donors read the site?

## Answered So Far

| Topic | Answer from the stakeholder |
| :--- | :--- |
| Signing in | Any visitor to the site can donate. No account or sign-in is needed. |
| Several campaigns | With more than one campaign, visitors see a list of them. With a single campaign, the site goes straight to it. |
| Smallest amount | As low as possible. The team checks what the payment gateway's documentation allows. |
| Suggested amounts and largest amount | The stakeholder asked the team to propose them. |
| Information asked for each type of donation | It depends on the type. For an invoice, the team works out the minimum information needed to issue one. |
| Proof of the donation | It depends on the type. The minimum is a ticket sent to the donor's email, and that includes anonymous donors. The team defines what it says. |
| Invoices | Tax deductions are in scope. Anyone who asks for a proper invoice should get one, not only companies. |
| Invoiced donors on the public list | Their donations must always be public, to prove the donation was made for SAT purposes. The team should look for edge cases. |
| Pending donations | A donation is pending only until the payment gateway authorizes it, so none stays pending indefinitely. The team reviews the gateway documentation for the details. |
| Campaign end date | Once the date has passed, the campaign stops receiving donations. There should always be at least one open campaign. |
| Campaign at 100% of its goal | There should be a way to mark whether the campaign keeps receiving donations. |
